# Episode 3 — Return to Sender (45-second review draft)

This is an isolated revision of Episode 3, not a new production episode. Existing
story checkpoints remain unchanged. The new reveal is proposed editorial content
and must not be silently written into the next episode's memory.

## Critical review before animation

The previous draft asserted that the lift knew her name without showing how;
mentioned yesterday's escape without establishing its connection to this new
courier; and ended with an unsupported claim about who sent her. It mostly
described images and let the protagonist watch events happen. The long countdown
and late face close-ups did not clearly connect evidence, decisions and outcomes.

The revised draft uses the reference's general techniques: a concrete impossible
detail, understandable physical geography, short spoken clauses, reaction before
the next escalation, and a reveal that changes the meaning of an earlier clue.
It uses none of the reference's distinctive story, dialogue or character designs.

The courier wants the lobby. An already-delivered parcel is the opening question.
She asks dispatch for help, tries the stop control, then keeps the parcel and
backs away when the doors reveal a stranger. Those are reasonable responses to
losing control. The figure's answer—she is the delivery—resolves the parcel's
misleading status while opening a specific question: how can she escape a handoff
that has already been accepted? It does not reveal the figure's face or explain
the larger mystery prematurely. The dialogue is intentionally brief under stress;
there is no name exposition, unexplained warning or sudden reckless challenge.

The floor still descends 13 to −1; the lower floor is sealed; the figure recognizes
the passenger; its face stays hidden. These existing facts and physical endpoints
are retained. The parcel/reveal is a new draft, not established checkpoint canon.

## Silent-story requirements

The hook label, LOBBY destination, descending display, failed stop press, SEALED
plaque, opening doors, protective grip, refusal palm, retreat and the figure's
point toward the person must communicate cause and reaction without narration.
The precise verbal twist still depends on dialogue: a silent viewer can read
that the stranger wants the courier, but cannot be assumed to infer every word
of the reveal. Review contact sheets for all shots; the short beginning/middle/end
clips alone cannot prove a fully edited film works silently.

## Preview plan, reuse and quality limits

Render only beginning 0–5 s, middle 17.5–22.5 s and ending 38–45 s at native
720×1280 / 24 FPS / 16 samples. Reuse the old middle display frames only after
checking their camera, floor sequence, lighting and image agreement with the new
scene. Changed hand/face shots must be rendered again. Reuse one validated black
plate for the deliberate final hold, never for a moving shot.

Repair captions from durations produced by the new speech take, with exact text
and audio-hash binding. Do not lower ASR thresholds to hide the previous eight
failures. Do not claim human listening approval from timing checks. Existing
local voice is temporary; narration expressiveness is secondary to this story pass.

The target of this review is coherent action and a meaningful cliffhanger. Human
editorial/listening review, subtle facial acting, convincing weight/foot contacts
and a later full native-HD film are still required before publication quality.

## Reproducing from a source-only checkout

Install Python 3.13 dependencies with
`python -m pip install -r requirements-production.txt -r requirements-audio.txt -r requirements-analysis.txt`.
Install Blender 5.2.3 and FFmpeg separately; neither executable is committed.
Run `python tools/setup_local_narrator.py` to explicitly download the free,
checksum-pinned candidate model to ignored local storage. Then use
`tools/review_episode3_story.py --blender <executable> --ffmpeg <executable>`
and `tools/render_story_review_clips.py --blender <executable> --ffmpeg <executable>`.
Both default to `artifacts/story_review/episode3_v4_reviewed`. Existing completed
revisions are protected; choose a fresh `--out` / matching `--source-dir` when
revising. The optional speech-recognition dependency is not used for v4 captions.

The older v3 render cache is optional. If it is absent, all required moving
frames are rendered; if present, its visual settings and probe pixels must
match before reuse. Partial frame jobs bind scene, renderer and profile hashes.
Completed preview receipts now bind the scene, audio, captions and MP4. Older
receipts without those fields remain historical evidence, but cannot authorize
silent reuse under the stricter resume check. No completed MP4 is overwritten
to upgrade a receipt. These local experiments do not unpause cloud production.
