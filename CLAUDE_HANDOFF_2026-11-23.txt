7. WRITING DIRECTION AND EPISODE 3 EDITORIAL STATUS
The user considers exact word choice, narrative coherence and suspense more
important than better voice synthesis. Writing must be interesting, clear and
motivated rather than a factual inventory of visible actions.

Approved overall voice structure:
1) Narrator: calm, confident, mysterious; context, motives and changed meaning.
2) Main character: natural emotional dialogue; decisions, questions, increasing
   fear where appropriate, not unnatural lines that fill time.

9. EXPLICIT SUSPENSE/INFORMATION MODEL ? IMPLEMENTED LOCALLY
Tracks:
- Audience knowledge versus protagonist knowledge and beliefs.
- Immediate goal, choices with reasons, later consequences and changing goals.
- When revelations occur, how beliefs are overturned and tension escalates.
- Why each narrator/dialogue line matters.
- Planted clues, protected late reveals and specific unanswered cliff questions.
